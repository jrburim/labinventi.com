Privacy in Legiro

Legiro, by LabInventi, offers guided reading without its own account, ads or cross-app tracking. This policy describes local processing and optional statistics in the current version.

Camera and reading

Camera controls are optional on compatible iPhone and iPad. Face Tracking and text recognition run on the device. Images, face meshes and facial signals are not recorded, stored or sent. Turn off the controls in the app or revoke camera permission in system Settings.

Data on your device

Preferences, bookmarks, reading history and imported PDF files stay on the device. The app and Safari share history and balance through the App Group. Deleting history does not delete your purchase or renew welcome minutes. The balance is not synced across devices.

Websites and cloud books

Websites you open in the browser have their own policies, connections, cookies and login sessions. Legiro opens the web reader for books you already have access to and adds guided reading. Recognition is transient and local; Legiro does not send book content to its own server.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through StoreKit. Legiro does not receive card details. A verified purchase entitlement is stored locally and shared with Safari, including for offline access. Apple lets you restore your purchase on another device; this does not transfer welcome minutes.

Optional statistics

Only with your consent, the iOS app sends TelemetryDeck in the European Union: session minutes in ranges, source type, cadence, camera on/off, error codes, balance warnings and exhaustion, word and usage-day ranges, purchase views and outcomes, welcome completion, app opens and goal changes. It never sends reading text, titles, full addresses, files, camera images or signals, credentials, account, location or IDFA.

Consent and retention

Statistics start off. Declining does not restrict reading or purchases. Enable or withdraw permission in Settings → Privacy and data. Turning it off cancels sending and discards the queue, which exists only in memory. The anonymous identifier changes every UTC day. Data already sent cannot be linked to you for individual deletion; the provider does not guarantee a deletion deadline for aggregate events. See TelemetryDeck’s policy and FAQ.

The store privacy disclosure includes product interactions and an anonymously hashed device identifier, not linked to you and not used for tracking.
